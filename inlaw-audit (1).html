The Problem

inlaw.au is blocking search engines

CRITICAL FINDING
Your homepage HTML contains meta-robots: noindex, nofollow
This single line of code tells Google, Bing, ChatGPT and every AI crawler: do not index this site, do not follow links from it. Inlaw is invisible to search by instruction — not by chance.
WHAT THIS MEANS
  • Zero organic Google traffic since launch in 2024
  • AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity cannot cite Inlaw
  • Your strongest proof — testimonials, named clients, the platform model — never reaches a buyer doing research
